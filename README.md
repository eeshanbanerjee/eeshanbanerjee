## Fancy seeing you here, stranger!

Hi! I'm Eeshan, currently pursuing a PhD in Computer Science, from Indian Statistical Institute, Kolkata. I'd love to give a firm set of characterisations about myself, using the topics I find interesting, and the hobbies I pursue in my spare time. But unfortunately, I'm just a complex human being, a bundle of contradictions, trying to figure life out one step at a time.

But in case such intimidation tactics are useless upon you, my current interests are Distribution Testing and Sublinear Algorithms. I had seriously pursued Olympiad Mathematics during my school days, and thus I use TA-ship as an excuse to teach students fun, nifty math bits.

Other than this, my hobbies are exactly the same as every other performative male:
- I love to watch films. Loved **There Will Be Blood** by PTA, but I also love films like **Swing Girls** or **The Hangover.**
- I can't imagine my life without music. My taste in music is quite diverse, everything in between TOOL and Bill Evans, and the occasional Jennifer Lopez listening spree.
- I play the Guitar sometimes. Trying to take things to the next level, but time management is a bitch.
- I have a complicated relationship with photography. But when I'm in the zone, I'm damn good at it.

That's all I have to say for now. Stick around, see if you find anything interesting. Stay safe!
